Your login stays with TrackMan

You sign in on TrackMan's own page. The app never sees your password, and iPhone Passwords can fill it in.

Signed in until you sign out

Your session is kept in the iPhone Keychain and renewed quietly, so you are not asked to log in every week.

No server in the middle

Data goes from TrackMan straight to your phone and stays there. Signing out deletes it.

The name

Training strokes

Every ball you hit on the range is a training stroke. Training Strokes counts them, shows what they turn into on the course, and makes it obvious when practice is paying off.
